A little about me

ABOUT

HI, I'm Tanvi

Design has always fascinated me as the bridge between what people need and what technology can offer. That fascination led me into UI/UX, where my journey has involved researching, sketching, and prototyping ideas into interfaces that feel effortless to use, while learning to balance user needs with business goals.

I find genuine satisfaction in simplifying complexity and shaping experiences that quietly do their job well. I am now open to both full time roles as a UI/UX designer and freelance collaborations, excited for what I get to design next.
